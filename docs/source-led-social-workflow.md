# Source-Led Social Workflow

Use this for AI updates, TikTok creator stories, LinkedIn posts, newsletters, and tool launches that should become LuliDigital or Fiker-led content.

## Principle

Do not fabricate relatability.

The source gives the direction. Fiker gives the point of view. LuliDigital gives the practical system.

## Inputs

- `source_notes`: what happened, in your own words.
- `source_url`: optional link for review.
- `fiker_take`: what Fiker thinks about it.
- `platform`: LinkedIn, Bluesky, blog, or short-form video.
- `audience`: business owners, founders, operators, creators, or curious builders.

## Rules

- Do not copy a creator's wording closely.
- Do not imply their story happened to Fiker.
- Do not invent clients, quotes, metrics, screenshots, personal history, or private details.
- Do not turn every update into hype.
- Keep the human angle in the interpretation, not in fake scenes.
- If the source is a personal creator story, extract the lesson or direction only.

## LinkedIn Shape

LinkedIn should sound credible, warm, and useful.

Structure:

1. What changed.
2. Why it matters for real business work.
3. Fiker or LuliDigital's point of view.
4. One practical next step.

## Bluesky Shape

Bluesky can be shorter, sharper, and more conversational.

Structure:

1. Direct reaction.
2. One useful implication.
3. Optional question or observation.

## Example

Source notes:

```text
A creator showed an AI agent completing a multi-step browser task. The exciting part was the speed. The risky part was how much judgment people were handing over without checks.
```

Fiker take:

```text
AI should carry the tedious work, but people should keep judgment, taste, and final approval.
```

LinkedIn draft:

```text
The most interesting AI updates are not the ones that make people disappear from the workflow.

They are the ones that move the boring middle faster.

Research, sorting, first drafts, follow-ups, summaries, checks. That work can be prepared by AI.

But judgment still needs a human.

The real opportunity for business owners is not "let AI do everything." It is building a system where AI prepares the work, and the person with taste, context, and responsibility makes the decision.
```

Bluesky draft:

```text
The useful AI shift is not replacing judgment.

It is removing the tedious work around judgment.

Let AI prepare. Let humans decide.
```
