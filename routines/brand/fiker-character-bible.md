# Fiker Character Bible (condensed for cloud routines)

Reference for Mini Fiker / Anime Fiker in scripts and asset briefs. Full
version lives in the local socials pipeline.

## Default identity (never change)
Warm brown skin · expressive almond-shaped brown eyes · defined brows ·
soft confident smile · short-to-medium curly copper-brown hair with
face-framing curls (THE brand identifier) · delicate gold earrings ·
polished creative-founder styling. Braids only as an alternate look for
travel/day-in-life scenes.

## Mini Fiker — professional business character
Use for: AI automation, AI employees, agency systems, client stories,
productivity, LinkedIn-safe thought leadership, buyer-facing content.
Look: black fitted top or clean neutral blouse, wide-leg dark jeans or
tailored trousers, optional light blazer; modern workspace scenes.
Personality: smart, direct, warm, curious; explains complicated systems
simply; confident without being corporate or cold.

Asset prompt template:
> Create a consistent animated character named Mini Fiker, a polished AI
> agency founder with warm brown skin, expressive almond-shaped brown eyes,
> defined brows, a soft confident smile, short-to-medium curly copper-brown
> hair with face-framing curls, delicate gold earrings, and a modern founder
> outfit: black fitted top, wide-leg dark jeans, clean belt, minimal
> jewelry. Style: premium 3D animated character, warm expressive face,
> modern creative business energy, clean lighting, social-media ready,
> vertical composition, high detail, friendly but credible.

## Anime Fiker — expressive entertainment character
Use for: founder humor, freelancer struggles, day-in-the-life, trend
reactions, relatable stories, emotional storytelling (incl. New Season).
Look: bigger expressions, bouncier curls, casual stylish outfits, dramatic
reaction poses, slightly exaggerated eyes; colorful but tasteful.
Personality: playful, witty, emotionally expressive — **smart friend
energy**; funny without becoming unserious.

Asset prompt template:
> Create a consistent anime-inspired character named Anime Fiker, a playful
> AI agency founder with warm brown skin, large expressive brown eyes,
> defined brows, a bright smile, short-to-medium bouncy curly copper-brown
> hair with face-framing curls, gold earrings, and stylish casual founder
> clothing. Style: modern anime / webtoon hybrid, expressive face, energetic
> body language, warm feminine creative energy, clean social-media
> illustration, vertical format, high detail, recognizable character design.
