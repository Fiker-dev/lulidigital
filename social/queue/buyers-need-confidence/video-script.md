# Video script — "Buyers don't need more content. They need confidence."

- Format: `full-size` (talking avatar, lip-sync + gestures)
- Character: Mini Fiker (business topic, buyer-facing)
- Voice preset: `trust` (buyer-facing authority, warm)
- Length target: ~33s, ~100 spoken words
- Poses (from public/assets/fiker-avatar-pack/): hook → `thinking`; build → `explaining`; turn → `lightbulb`; payoff → `confidence`; CTA → `pointing-right`

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
Spoken: "When a deal goes quiet, more content is almost never the fix."
Caption overlay: A STALLED DEAL ≠ NEEDS MORE CONTENT
Pose: thinking

**3–13s — BUILD (the common mistake)**
Spoken: "The instinct is to add. Another case study. A longer deck. One more explainer for the objection you think they have. It feels responsible — more proof, more reasons to pick you."
Caption overlay: the instinct: ADD MORE / proof · decks · explainers
Pose: explaining

**13–24s — TURN (the insight)**
Spoken: "But buyers aren't short on information. They're drowning in it. What they're short on is confidence — the sense that choosing you is a safe call they won't have to defend later."
Caption overlay: not short on INFO → short on CONFIDENCE
Pose: lightbulb

**24–31s — PAYOFF**
Spoken: "So make the decision clearer, not fuller. Who it's for, what happens next, the price and the risk — said plainly, before they ask."
Caption overlay: clearer, not fuller / easiest to say yes to wins
Pose: confidence

**31–33s — CTA**
Spoken: "More at lulidigital.com."
Caption overlay: lulidigital.com
Pose: pointing-right
