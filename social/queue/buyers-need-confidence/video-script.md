# Your buyers need confidence

**Pillar:** Agency Life / buyer education  
**Platform:** LinkedIn personal  
**Length:** approximately 17 seconds  
**Character:** Mini Fiker  
**Format:** 9:16 caption-led motion video using thinking → explaining → confidence → thumbsup

## Retention structure

- **0–4s — Hook:** Your buyers aren't confused. They're afraid of making the wrong choice.
- **4–8s — Tension:** More content won't fix that fear. Information is everywhere. Confidence isn't.
- **8–12s — Reframe:** AI made comparison easy. Make the decision feel safe.
- **12–16s — Payoff:** Show the process. Name the trade-offs. Clarity beats volume.

Sound design stays LinkedIn-safe: a soft opening impact, restrained transition
whooshes, and a quiet closing chime. The voice remains dominant and there is no
background music competing with the message.

Voice-over is cloned with Fiker's explicit consent directly from
`~/Desktop/myoutreachvid.MP4` using the local Chatterbox model. The captions
still carry the complete idea, so the post also works muted in the LinkedIn
feed. No paid animation or Hedra credits are required.

## Trend grounding

- LinkedIn and Bain's 2026 “Buyability” research frames stalled B2B decisions as a confidence problem, including fear of making the wrong choice.
- LinkedIn's current B2B video guidance emphasizes short, vertical, story-led video and relatable voices over polished corporate monologues.
