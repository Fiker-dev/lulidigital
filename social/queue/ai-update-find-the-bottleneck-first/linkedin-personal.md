A startup just raised $20M — with Marc Benioff backing it — to solve a problem most founders won't say out loud.

Not "which AI model is best." The quieter one: you bought the AI, and you still don't know where to point it.

June (launched earlier this month, per TechCrunch) does one clever thing. Before it automates anything, it scans how your business actually runs — the real steps, the handoffs, the places work piles up — and only then builds an agent for the process that's genuinely costing you. Find the bottleneck first. Automate second.

That order is the whole game. And you don't need their platform to use it.

Here's how you can actually use this today, for free:

- For one week, jot down every task you personally touch that a system could have done — the follow-up email, the invoice reminder, the "can you send me that again."
- Pick the ONE that shows up most and drains the most attention. That's your bottleneck — not the flashiest thing to automate.
- Automate that single process end to end before you touch anything else.

Most AI experiments don't fail because the tool was weak. They fail because it got handed a vague job inside a workflow nobody mapped. Map the workflow, and even a simple agent earns its keep.

The companies pulling ahead with AI aren't the ones with the best model. They're the ones who knew exactly where to put it.
