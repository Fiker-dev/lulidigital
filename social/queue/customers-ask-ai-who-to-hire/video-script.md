# Video Script — Your Customers Are Asking AI Who to Hire

**Format:** `full-size` (Mini Fiker talking direct-to-camera, lip-sync + gestures)
**Character:** Mini Fiker (business topic — AI visibility / buyer discovery, buyer-facing)
**Duration:** ~38s vertical short
**Voice preset:** `trust` (buyer-facing, calm authority)
**Weekly theme:** Monday — Educational (counter-intuitive framework, no pitch)

---

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
- Spoken: "Your next client just asked AI who to hire. Did it say your name?"
- Caption overlay: `They asked AI who to hire.`
- Pose: `phone` (holding phone, direct to camera)

**3–14s — CURIOSITY GAP / SETUP**
- Spoken: "Because it doesn't hand back ten links anymore. It names one or two, gives a reason, and moves on. You're in that answer, or you're invisible — and you never find out."
- Caption overlay: `No page two. Named or invisible.`
- Pose: `explaining` (direct, leaning in)

**14–30s — THE FRAMEWORK (payoff)**
- Spoken: "Here's what people get wrong. You don't fix this by polishing your homepage. AI runs on consensus — it won't just take your word for it. What gets you named is being vouched for somewhere else: a real review, an accurate listing, a mention you didn't write."
- Caption overlay (cycle): `Not your homepage.` → `AI runs on consensus.` → `Get vouched for elsewhere.`
- Pose: `lightbulb` (the reframe landing)

**30–38s — CTA**
- Spoken: "So go ask ChatGPT who to hire for what you do. Then go get corroborated."
- Caption overlay: `Ask it today. More at lulidigital.com`
- Pose: `confidence` (settled, direct close)

---

## Notes
- **Word count (spoken):** ~108 words — within the ≤110 limit.
- **B-roll:** none — `full-size` talking avatar throughout. Mini Fiker is the only face (brand rule).
- **Overlay style:** honey-brand caption cards, one idea per beat; the framework section animates in as a three-line stack (viewers watch muted).
- **Grounding:** the "consensus / get vouched for" point reflects the Princeton GEO research (KDD 2024) — biggest visibility gains came from cited, corroborated substance, not marketing copy. No on-screen stat claims; kept conceptual so nothing needs a citation card.
- **Blog link:** first comment on LinkedIn, never in the caption. Native video only.
- **Render:** local avatar-animator pipeline (MuseTalk/LivePortrait). LinkedIn upload stays manual.
