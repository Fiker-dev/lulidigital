Your last AI experiment didn't die because the tool was bad. MIT looked at 300+ company rollouts and found 95% delivered no return — and the models were fine. The tool just never got wired into the real work. A demo and a normal Tuesday are two different things.

--- POST 2 ---
The fix isn't a better tool. Pick one task you'd love to never do again, hand it the messy 80%, keep the final say, put one number on it for two weeks. If the number moves, keep it. That's sticky AI — one boring job at a time.
