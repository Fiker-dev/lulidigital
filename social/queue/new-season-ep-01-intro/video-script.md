# Video Script — New Season, Episode 1: "Hey, I'm Fiker"

- **Series:** New Season (founder story) — EPISODE 1 / signature intro
- **Format:** `mini-irl` — small Anime Fiker walking through REAL footage,
  Disney-narrator energy: expressive walk cycle, big presenting hand
  gestures, one gesture per idea
- **Character:** Anime Fiker (smart friend energy, bouncy curls, bright)
- **Duration:** ~45s vertical
- **Voice preset:** `expressive`
- **Total spoken words:** ~105

---

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
Spoken: "I used to put people to sleep for a living. Now I build things
that work while you sleep."
Overlay: `I PUT PEOPLE TO SLEEP FOR A LIVING`
Footage: hospital corridor b-roll (Pexels: "hospital corridor walking").
Mini Fiker walks INTO frame along the corridor floor, waves at camera.

**3–12s — WHO I AM**
Spoken: "Hey, I'm Fiker. I trained as a nurse anaesthetist — years of
checklists, calm under pressure, and getting the details right when it
actually matters."
Overlay: `NURSE ANAESTHETIST →` / `CHECKLISTS. CALM. PRECISION.`
Footage: continue hospital/medical b-roll. Mini Fiker walks steadily,
counts the three words off on her fingers (Disney-presenting gesture).

**12–24s — THE TURN**
Spoken: "But I kept building things. Little systems. Websites. Automations.
And one day I realised — the same discipline that keeps a patient safe is
exactly what makes technology you can trust."
Overlay: `THE SAME DISCIPLINE` → `TECHNOLOGY YOU CAN TRUST`
Footage: transition to city-walking b-roll (Pexels: "city walking feet
morning"). Mini Fiker hops from the hospital scene into the street scene —
the visual chapter break. Idea-lightbulb expression on "realised".

**24–36s — THE NEW SEASON**
Spoken: "So I started a new season. I run LuliDigital now — we build AI
systems, marketing, and support for founders who want things done properly.
Same standards. Different theatre."
Overlay: `NEW SEASON` → `SAME STANDARDS. DIFFERENT THEATRE.`
Footage: desk/laptop/studio b-roll (Pexels: "laptop desk creative studio").
Mini Fiker lands at the desk scene, proud-shipped-it expression, arms open
on "properly".

**36–45s — SERIES CTA**
Spoken: "I'm documenting all of it here — what I learn, what I build, and
what actually works. This is episode one. Come along."
Overlay: `EPISODE 1` → `COME ALONG` → `lulidigital.com`
Footage: hold desk scene. Mini Fiker sits on the edge of the laptop
(sitting-perch pose), pats the spot next to her, confident smile.

---

## Production notes
- Real footage: 3 clips (hospital corridor / city walking / desk studio) —
  all Pexels-safe searches listed above. Keep each clip's motion slow so the
  walk composite reads clearly.
- Walk cycle + the three key gestures (finger-count, lightbulb, arms-open)
  become REUSABLE library assets — build once, every future mini-irl video
  gets faster.
- Captions on, one line at a time, high contrast.
- Facts check: healthcare training + nurse anaesthetist + runs LuliDigital —
  all true; no invented anecdotes, no numbers we can't back.
- Tone guard: the past is the training ground, never the thing escaped.
  "Same standards, different theatre" is the thesis of the whole series.
