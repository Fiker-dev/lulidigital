## Why this format

The feed asset uses one strong, useful statement instead of reproducing the full article title. It is designed as a native visual that can stand alone, with the company caption supplying the operational context. The 4:5 portrait ratio gives the first image greater visual emphasis in LinkedIn's current feed while staying inside LinkedIn's supported 3:1-to-4:5 range.

## Verified platform guidance

- LinkedIn Help, “Share photos on LinkedIn”: recommends images at least 1080 px wide, supports ratios up to 4:5, and notes that 4:5 gives the first image greater visual emphasis: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a527229/sharing-photos-or-videos
- LinkedIn Pages Best Practices: recommends mixing text and image formats and using Page content to help professionals stay informed: https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/linkedin-pages/lms-linkedin-page-posting-best-practices-one-pager.pdf
