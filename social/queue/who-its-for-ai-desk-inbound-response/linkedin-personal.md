Every enquiry your business gets is queued behind one person: you.

But you can't sit on the form all day, and hiring someone to watch it is a lot of money for work that's 80% pattern.

That's the exact shape the AI Desk was built for. Something that reads each enquiry the moment it lands, works out what it actually is, drafts a real reply in your voice, and books the call — then hands it to you to approve before a word goes out. You're not removed from the loop. You're just no longer the reason it's slow.

The test is simple: if a lead came in right now, how long until they heard back? If the honest answer is "tomorrow," that's your gap.
