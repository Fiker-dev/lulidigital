Entering a New EU Market? Build the Operational Blueprint Before You Build the Campaign

"The biggest risk in EU market entry isn't moving too slowly. It's moving fast in the wrong direction."

Most founders expanding into Germany, Denmark, Switzerland, or Norway start with a translated landing page and some ads. Six months later the budget is half gone and the data tells them nothing useful. The problem isn't the market. It's the sequence. Campaigns launched before the operational foundation is in place produce noise, not signal.

Before any AI tool touches content or targeting, three questions need answers: Where do buyers in this specific market actually spend time online? What language and register do they use in their own sector? What does the local competitive landscape look like — not the global one? That audit shapes everything that follows. Skip it and even fluent, well-produced content lands with the cultural weight of a tourist brochure.

The full piece walks through the audit-first sequence, what to automate versus what always needs a human sign-off, and how to keep fixed costs low while a new market proves itself.
