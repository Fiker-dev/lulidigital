## Production notes
- Captions ON by default (most viewers watch muted); one line per beat, high contrast.
- No stock footage needed — `full-size` talking avatar only.
- Keep the Air Canada reference factual and neutral; it is a real 2024 tribunal ruling (Moffatt v. Air Canada, BC Civil Resolution Tribunal), not a hypothetical.
- Renders locally via the avatar-animator pipeline (MuseTalk / LivePortrait).
