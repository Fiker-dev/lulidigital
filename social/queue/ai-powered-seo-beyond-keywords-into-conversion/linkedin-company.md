AI-Powered SEO: Beyond Keywords, Into Conversion

"Ranking is a vanity metric. Ranking on pages your buyers actually use is a business strategy."

Most founders optimise for traffic. The actual problem is usually a stage mismatch — your content is written for one part of the funnel while the majority of your visitors arrive at a completely different one. Before publishing another piece, it is worth knowing which pages attract high-intent visitors who leave without acting, and why.

The practical move is an intent audit. Run your core topics through an AI-assisted clustering tool and look at which pages sit in positions 4 to 15 with strong impressions but low clicks. Those are your fastest wins. Then check your highest-traffic pages against actual conversion data. Traffic without intent is a cost, not an asset — and AI compresses the analysis that used to take weeks into something you can act on in hours.

The full piece walks through intent analysis, content gap identification, competitor deconstruction, conversion path diagnosis, and how to connect SEO reporting directly to pipeline — not just position.
