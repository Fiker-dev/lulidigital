The Onboarding Gap That's Quietly Costing You Clients

"The gap between 'signed' and 'confident' is where retention is won or lost."

Most client churn doesn't happen at month six. It happens in the first 30 days, while attention is already on the next sale. A welcome email goes out, a kickoff call gets promised, and then the handoff quietly falls apart. Three weeks in, a new client is already second-guessing the decision — and you don't know it yet.

Before adding any new tool, run a one-hour audit on what you already have. Write out exactly what a new client receives in their first seven days. Map who owns each onboarding step. If the answer to either question is vague, that's where clients are going quiet on you. The gaps are almost always obvious once they're on paper — they're just rarely looked at.

The full piece walks through how to automate onboarding triggers, build a proactive retention system, and decide what should stay in human hands.
