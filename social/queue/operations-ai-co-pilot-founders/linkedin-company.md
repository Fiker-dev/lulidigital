Your Operations Are Running You. Here's How to Flip That.

"The goal isn't to replace human judgment. It's to make sure human judgment is the only thing humans are doing."

Most founders didn't sign up to spend their best hours reformatting data or manually triggering workflows a system could handle in seconds. But that's where a lot of them are. The problem isn't the tools — it's that nobody handed them a clear operational map before they started reaching for AI.

Before automating anything, run a simple triage. List every task your team repeats weekly. Ask two questions about each one: does it require original thinking, or does it follow a rule? And if it went wrong, would a human need to catch it before it caused damage? Sort your answers into three buckets — automate fully, automate with approval, or keep human-led. Most founders are surprised how much lands in that first bucket. That's exactly where to start.

The full piece covers how to audit your operations, which workflows are worth automating first, and what a human should still own — no matter how clean the system gets.
