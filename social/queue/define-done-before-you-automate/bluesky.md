POST 1:
Most automations don't fail because the tool is weak. They fail because nobody wrote down what "done right" looks like before switching it on. When a person ran the process, they filled the gaps silently. Automate it and the gaps don't vanish — they just stop being handled.

POST 2:
So before automating anything, write the process down by hand: the trigger, every step, the decision points, the one spot a mistake would hurt. If you can't say what "finished correctly" means in a sentence, the tool can't either. More at lulidigital.com
