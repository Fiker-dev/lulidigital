Most automations don't fail because the tool is weak. They fail because nobody wrote down what "done right" looks like before switching it on. Before you automate anything, spend ten minutes writing the finish line — the standard, not the steps. 🧵

---

"Sort my inbox" → a pile you re-check. "Client mail to Priority, flag anything about money or a deadline, leave the rest" → something you trust by 8am. The finish line is the whole job. Write it once, hand the machine that. More: lulidigital.com
