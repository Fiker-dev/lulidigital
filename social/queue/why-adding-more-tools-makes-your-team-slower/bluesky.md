**Post 1/3**
Every tool you bought made sense on its own. That's why the pile got so big. The cost was never the subscriptions — it's the gaps between them: copy-paste, the same figure retyped in three apps, someone checking it synced. Busy all day, never caught up.

**Post 2/3**
Wild part: in a 2025 study, experienced devs given AI tools finished ~19% slower — but felt faster. The feeling of speed and the fact of it came apart. Small teams live in that same gap, one login at a time.

**Post 3/3**
The fix isn't one perfect tool. It's wiring the ones you already trust to hand work to each other. Map how one job moves. Find the ugliest copy-paste. Connect just that seam. Watch what a week frees up. lulidigital.com/blog/why-adding-more-tools-makes-your-team-slower
