Swiss SMBs Are Being Sold "AI Marketing." Here's How to Tell If It's Real.

"If an agency cannot explain their AI workflow in plain language, they do not have one."

Before you sign anything, ask one simple question: which specific tasks does the AI handle, and which does a human review before it goes live? A real answer is quick and operational. A vague one about "proprietary technology" is the answer.

The other thing worth knowing: most AI marketing tools are built for single-language markets. Switzerland is not that. German, French, and Italian carry different buyer registers and different content rhythms. Any agency claiming AI-led capability here should show you how their system handles that complexity — not just run copy through a translation layer and call it localised.

The full piece covers the real spectrum of what "AI marketing" means, the red flags to watch for in a pitch, and the six questions to bring into any agency conversation before you commit.
