## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
- Spoken: "Somewhere this year, you became the bottleneck in your own business."
- Caption overlay: `YOU BECAME THE BOTTLENECK`

- Spoken: "Write the five things only you know. That's your bottleneck. More at lulidigital.com."
