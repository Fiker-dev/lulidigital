New on the blog: German B2B Marketing Is Still Mostly Manual. That's the Gap AI Fills.

Most German B2B teams aren't slow because they lack effort. They're slow because the operational layer — lead lists, follow-up sequences, fortnightly reports — is still run by hand, and that drag compounds every week.

"The goal is not to remove your team from marketing. It is to stop your team from being buried by it."

The piece lays out a four-layer audit (content, lead routing, nurture, reporting), what to automate first, and — just as important for the DACH market — what should always keep a human in the loop: outbound to named accounts, brand-sensitive content, and anything touching GDPR-consented data.
