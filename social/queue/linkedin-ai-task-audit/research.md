# LinkedIn high-performer research

Research date: 2026-08-25

## What current high performers are doing

- Publishing from a person, not only a company page. Metricool's 2026 study found personal profiles averaged 63% higher engagement than company pages.
- Designing for private actions such as clicks and carousel swipes, not just public likes. Overall engagement rose nearly 14% even as likes, comments, and shares fell.
- Using carousels and multi-image posts for structured, saveable ideas. Metricool found both formats outperform standard image and video posts across most key metrics.
- Leading with a specific first-person observation. A July 2025 to July 2026 analysis of 130,000 posts reported a 20% lift when posts opened with “I,” “my,” or “we” and immediately said what happened.
- Keeping each slide to one thought. The asset uses a narrow audit, strong sequencing, and a useful takeaway rather than a broad “tips” list.
- Making expertise conversational. LinkedIn's analysis of 13,000 B2B videos found expert-led, personal delivery outperformed formal corporate delivery; the same voice principle is applied to the caption.

## Format decision

Use an eight-page native PDF carousel on Fiker's personal profile. This creates swipe depth, supports saves, and gives the practical framework enough room without turning it into a long post. The design follows the established LuliDigital dark-ink and honey visual system.
