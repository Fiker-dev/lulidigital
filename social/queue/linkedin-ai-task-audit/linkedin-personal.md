I stopped asking, “What can AI do?”

I started asking a better question:

Where is work waiting for a decision?

That changed the way I look at automation.

The task itself is often not the bottleneck. Work gets stuck because someone still needs to choose, approve, route, chase, or check something before it can move.

So I use a five-minute audit:

1. Find what repeats at least three times a week.
2. Find the decisions that already follow a clear rule.
3. Mark the moments that still need human judgment.
4. Score the rest by frequency, rule clarity, and cost of delay.

The highest score is usually a better place to start than the most impressive AI tool.

Good automation does not remove judgment. It protects judgment for the moments that deserve it.
