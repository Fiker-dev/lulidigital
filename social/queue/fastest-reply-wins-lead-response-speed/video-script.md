# Video Script — The Fastest Reply Usually Wins

**Format:** `full-size` (Mini Fiker talking direct-to-camera, lip-sync + gestures)
**Character:** Mini Fiker (business topic — speed-to-lead / marketing operations, buyer-facing)
**Duration:** ~40s vertical short
**Voice preset:** `trust` (buyer-facing, calm authority)
**Weekly theme:** Value Tip lean — one actionable idea, earned soft mention of the fix

---

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
- Spoken: "The reply that wins the client usually isn't the smartest one. It's the fastest."
- Caption overlay: `First beats best.`
- Pose: `phone` (holding phone, direct to camera)

**3–15s — CURIOSITY GAP / SETUP**
- Spoken: "A new enquiry has a shelf life shorter than a lunch break. Harvard studied over a million leads — reply within an hour and you're nearly seven times more likely to reach the decision-maker. Wait a day, sixty times less likely."
- Caption overlay (cycle): `Reply in 1 hr = ~7x` → `Wait a day = 60x worse`
- Pose: `explaining` (leaning in, direct)

**15–31s — THE REFRAME (payoff)**
- Spoken: "Here's what founders get wrong. This looks like a lead problem, so they buy more traffic. It's a response problem in disguise — every new lead waits on you being free at a random moment. You never are. Willpower can't beat the clock. Only a system can."
- Caption overlay (cycle): `Not a lead problem.` → `A response problem.` → `Fix the leak, not the volume.`
- Pose: `lightbulb` (the reframe landing)

**31–40s — CTA**
- Spoken: "Wire one instant reply the second a form comes in. Put a one-hour clock on the human follow-up. Answer while it's still warm."
- Caption overlay: `Instant ack + 1-hour clock. More at lulidigital.com`
- Pose: `confidence` (settled, direct close)

---

## Notes
- **Word count (spoken):** ~104 words — within the ≤110 limit.
- **B-roll:** none — `full-size` talking avatar throughout. Mini Fiker is the only face (brand rule).
- **Overlay style:** honey-brand caption cards, one idea per beat; the stat beat animates the two figures in sequence, the reframe beat as a three-line stack (viewers watch muted).
- **Grounding:** HBR "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011, 1.25M leads) — ~7x within an hour, 60x within a day. Both figures verified via web search; safe to show on-screen as stat cards. The 5-minute MIT figure is kept out of the spoken script to avoid stacking two studies in 40s.
- **Blog link:** first comment on LinkedIn, never in the caption. Native video only.
- **Render:** local avatar-animator pipeline (MuseTalk/LivePortrait). LinkedIn upload stays manual — Fiker posts and stays for the first hour.
