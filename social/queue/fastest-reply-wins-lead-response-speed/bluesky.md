**Post 1/3**

**Post 2/3**
HBR studied 1.25M leads: reply within an hour and you're ~7x more likely to reach a decision-maker than waiting even 60 minutes longer. An MIT study found 5 minutes beats 30 by a mile. The reply that wins usually isn't the cleverest one. It's the first one.

**Post 3/3**
