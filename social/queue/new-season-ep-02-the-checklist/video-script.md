# Video Script — New Season, Episode 2: "The Checklist"

- **Series:** New Season (founder story) — EPISODE 2
- **Format:** `mini-irl` — small Anime Fiker composited over REAL footage,
  walking and presenting Disney-narrator energy: one gesture per idea. This
  episode carries a visual "hospital → desk" chapter turn like Episode 1, so
  it reads as the same series.
- **Character:** Anime Fiker (smart-friend energy, bouncy curls, expressive)
- **Duration:** ~40s vertical
- **Voice preset:** `expressive`
- **Total spoken words:** ~102

---

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
Spoken: "Before I ever put anyone under, I ran the same checklist. Every
single time."
Overlay: `SAME CHECKLIST. EVERY TIME.`
Footage: pre-op / hospital prep b-roll — gloved hands laying out
instruments, monitors (Pexels: "hospital monitor hands", "surgical
instruments tray"). NO faces. Anime Fiker walks into frame, `waving` pose.

**3–13s — WHAT THE CHECK IS**
Spoken: "Identity. Allergies. Airway. Is the suction working. You do it even
when you're certain — because certain is exactly when people skip a step."
Overlay: `IDENTITY · ALLERGIES · AIRWAY` → `EVEN WHEN YOU'RE SURE`
Footage: continue medical prep b-roll — hands checking equipment, IV line,
oxygen gauge. Anime Fiker counts the items off on her fingers (`explaining`
pose), steady walk.

**13–24s — THE TURN**
Spoken: "I build systems now, not anaesthesia. But that habit came with me —
and it's the most valuable thing I own."
Overlay: `I BUILD SYSTEMS NOW` → `SAME HABIT`
Footage: chapter break — hop from hospital prep into a desk/studio scene
(Pexels: "laptop desk morning", "hands typing keyboard"). Anime Fiker jumps
scenes on "now", `lightbulb` expression on "habit came with me".

**24–34s — THE PAYOFF**
Spoken: "Nothing ships to a client until it's checked. What breaks at 2am.
Where's the rollback. The failure they'll never see — because I planned for
it. That's why people trust the work."
Overlay: `WHAT BREAKS AT 2AM?` → `THE FAILURE THEY NEVER SEE`
Footage: desk b-roll — screen with a dashboard/checklist, hand ticking items
(Pexels: "checklist pen paper", "dashboard screen"). Anime Fiker at the desk,
`confidence` pose, `notebook` in hand on "checked".

**34–40s — SERIES CTA**
Spoken: "You don't need another tool. You need a checklist you won't skip.
Same standards. Different theatre. Episode two."
Overlay: `NOT ANOTHER TOOL` → `SAME STANDARDS. DIFFERENT THEATRE.` →
`EPISODE 2 · lulidigital.com`
Footage: hold desk scene. Anime Fiker sits on the edge of the laptop
(`sitting-perch` pose), confident smile, small nod.

## Production notes
- Real footage: reuse the desk/studio clips from Episode 1 where possible so
  the series looks consistent; add 2 medical-prep clips (instrument tray,
  monitor/IV hands). All Pexels-safe, NO recognisable faces — Anime Fiker is
  the only face.
- Reuses the Episode 1 walk cycle + finger-count + scene-hop gestures
  (already built as library assets). Only new asset needed: `notebook`
  pose at desk — already in the avatar pack.
- Captions on, one line at a time, high contrast; most viewers watch muted.
- Fact guard: nurse anaesthetist pre-op checks (WHO Surgical Safety Checklist
  sign-in — identity, allergies, airway/aspiration risk, equipment) are real
  and accurately stated. The tech parallels (production readiness reviews,
  evals, pre-mortems) are real, documented practices. No invented anecdotes,
  no numbers we can't back, no named patient stories.
- Tone guard: healthcare is the training ground, never the thing escaped.
  "Same standards, different theatre" ties Episode 2 to Episode 1.
