Picture a jet engine bolted to the ceiling to move a bit of air. That's how most of us are still using AI.

Ask a question, get an answer, copy it into an email, move on. It's useful. It's also the smallest thing the technology does.

The real difference shows up when AI runs the whole path instead of one step of it: it reads the incoming email, works out what it is, routes it, drafts the reply in your voice, and books the follow-up. Your job shrinks to a yes or a no.

Same tools everyone already has. The gap isn't the model — it's whether you gave it the full job or just a question.

Start with one workflow you repeat every week. Write down what you actually do, decisions included. That's the part that turns a clever answer into work that's already done when you wake up.
