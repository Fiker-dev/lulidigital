# Video script — "Go back to the maybes"

- **Format:** full-size (Mini Fiker, direct-to-camera, lip-sync + gestures)
- **Character:** Mini Fiker (business topic — lead reactivation)
- **Voice preset:** trust (buyer-facing)
- **Length target:** ~35s, ≤110 spoken words
- **Pose/scene:** open on "explaining" pose (avatar pack), shift to "counting on fingers" for the steps, close on soft confident smile

---

**0–3s — HOOK**
Spoken: "You don't need more leads. You need to go back to the ones who already said maybe."
Caption overlay: YOU DON'T NEED MORE LEADS

**3–10s — CURIOSITY GAP**
Spoken: "There's a list you already own and never work — the people who asked once, then went quiet. 'No reply' isn't 'no.' It's usually just timing."
Caption overlay: "NO REPLY" ≠ "NO"

**10–24s — THE HOW**
Spoken: "Sort them by how far they got, not how recent. Someone who saw a proposal beats someone who filled in a form. Lead with their problem, not 'just checking in.' A few touches, then let them rest."

**24–32s — PAYOFF**
Spoken: "The reason you skip it isn't strategy. It's boring and never urgent — so make it a system, not a willpower thing."
Caption overlay: MAKE IT A SYSTEM, NOT A WILLPOWER THING

**32–35s — CTA**
Spoken: "Before you buy another lead, count your maybes."
Caption overlay: More at lulidigital.com
