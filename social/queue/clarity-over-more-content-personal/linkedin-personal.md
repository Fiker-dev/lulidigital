# I used to think good marketing meant saying more

More posts. More detail. More reasons for someone to choose you.

But building LuliDigital has taught me something different:

More information can create more hesitation.

People rarely need another wall of content. They need enough clarity to feel confident about their next step.

That means:

- Showing how the process actually works
- Explaining the trade-offs honestly
- Making expectations clear
- Removing unnecessary friction from the decision

My rule now is simple: the goal is not to say more. It is to make the decision clearer.

What helps you feel confident before choosing a business or service?
