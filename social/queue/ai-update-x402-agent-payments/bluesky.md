**Post 1/2**
Last week the Linux Foundation launched a standard that lets AI agents pay for things themselves. Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Shopify and Coinbase all signed on.

For two years "agent" meant it recommends and you click. This is the plumbing for the version that clicks itself.

**Post 2/2**
Not a reason to panic. A reason to draw one line now: where would you let software spend money, and where never?

Reversible + capped → an agent can, inside a limit. Contracts + suppliers → you sign. Decide it before spending goes frictionless.
