# Video script — "You're not underpriced. You're under-explained."

- Format: full-size (talking avatar, lip-sync + gestures — buyer-facing authority)
- Character: Mini Fiker (business topic)
- Voice preset: trust
- Target length: ~32s, ~95 words spoken
- Pillar: 2 — Freelancer Reality (pricing / positioning)

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
Spoken: "Most deals you lose on price were never about price."
Caption overlay: LOST ON PRICE? PROBABLY NOT.
Pose/scene: `confidence.png` — direct to camera, still.

**3–10s — CURIOSITY GAP**
Spoken: "The buyer couldn't tell what they were actually getting. So they compared numbers. And a number always loses to a smaller number."
Caption overlay: FUZZY OUTCOME = PRICE WAR
Pose/scene: `thinking.png` — slight lean in.

**10–22s — BUILD / PAYOFF**
Spoken: "The fix isn't a discount. It's making the outcome impossible to misread. Say what they'll have at the end in plain words. What changes for them. And what the old way keeps costing them."
Caption overlays (cycle): WHAT THEY'LL HAVE · WHAT CHANGES · WHAT IT COSTS TO WAIT
Pose/scene: `explaining.png` → `laptop.png` on the three items.

**22–32s — LESSON + CTA**
Spoken: "Do that and price becomes a line item on a decision they've already made. You're probably not underpriced. You're under-explained. More at lulidigital dot com."
Caption overlay: UNDER-EXPLAINED, NOT UNDERPRICED → lulidigital.com
Pose/scene: `thumbsup.png` — warm close.

- No fabricated stats, no client names — evergreen.
