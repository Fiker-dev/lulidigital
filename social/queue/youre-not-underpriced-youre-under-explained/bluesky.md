Post 1:
Most deals you think you lost on price, you lost on clarity. The buyer couldn't picture what they were getting, so they compared numbers — and a number always loses to a smaller one.

Post 2:
The fix isn't a discount. It's making the outcome impossible to misread: what they'll have, what changes for them, what the old way keeps costing. You're probably not underpriced. You're under-explained.
