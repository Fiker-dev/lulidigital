# Video Script — The First Two Weeks Decide Retention

**Format:** `full-size` (Mini Fiker talking direct-to-camera, lip-sync + gestures)
**Character:** Mini Fiker (business topic — client retention / operations)
**Duration:** ~38s vertical short
**Voice preset:** `trust` (buyer-facing, calm authority)
**Weekly theme:** Wednesday — Value Tip

---

## Beats

**0–3s — HOOK**
- Spoken: "Every client you've lost, you probably lost in the first two weeks."
- Caption overlay: `You lose clients in week one.`
- Pose: `explaining` (direct, leaning in)

**3–12s — CURIOSITY GAP / SETUP**
- Spoken: "Not because the work was bad. Because they can't judge the work yet — it hasn't started. So they judge the only thing they can see: how calm those first days feel."
- Caption overlay: `They judge the start, not the work.`
- Pose: `thinking` (hand near chin, considering)

**12–24s — THE TIP (payoff)**
- Spoken: "Fix it once. Build the first week as a checklist: one warm welcome that bundles every ask, one visible win inside seven days, one owner who runs it every time."
- Caption overlay (cycle): `1. One welcome` → `2. One win in 7 days` → `3. One owner`
- Pose: `laptop` (building the system)

**24–34s — REVEAL / WHY IT WORKS**
- Spoken: "Costs you an afternoon to set up. After that, every client starts on the same calm footing — instead of whatever version you had energy for that week."
- Caption overlay: `An afternoon once. Runs itself after.`
- Pose: `thumbsup` (settled, confident)

**34–38s — CTA**
- Spoken: "That's what keeps them."
- Caption overlay: `More at lulidigital.com`
- Pose: `explaining` (soft close)

---

## Notes
- **Word count (spoken):** ~92 words — within the ≤110 limit.
- **B-roll:** none — `full-size` talking avatar throughout. No human faces other than Mini Fiker (brand rule).
- **Overlay style:** honey-brand caption cards, one idea per beat; the three-step section animates in as a numbered stack (viewers watch muted).
- **Blog link:** first comment on LinkedIn, never in the caption. Native video only.
- **Render:** local avatar-animator pipeline (MuseTalk/LivePortrait). LinkedIn upload stays manual.
