!AI Update — the wildcard

News can't be calendared. When a real, verified AI development lands, it becomes Wednesday's video in place of the Value Tip (or jumps the queue same-day if it's big). No news that week → the default runs. Not to be confused with Trending — that's a vibe, this is verified news.

Making a note / quote post

Three tiers, by how personal it is

1real hand

Real handwriting

Sticky note or torn paper, your hand, shot in your space. Imperfection is the credibility. → Shot on iPhone, post direct.

2template

Branded paper

Remotion "LULIDIGITAL TIMES" look, consistent hand-font + luli mark. Batch several. → Render on Mac.

3rare

Billboard / flyer

One punchy line staged in the real world. Novelty — use sparingly.

From screen to feed

Mac → iPhone

Real notes: already on your phone — post straight from it.

Rendered: AirDrop for one-offs (best quality), iCloud for batches → Photos on phone.

Always: post video & images from the phone; paste the caption from the pack.

Got a spontaneous idea?

Capture → brainstorm → output

Who you tell: Amara, your Social Team agent.

1

Capture

Park one raw line in ideas-inbox.md. Don't polish, don't lose it.

2

Brainstorm with Amara

Open a session — "Amara, let's work this" — or her next run picks it from the inbox. Pick the bucket, tier, hook, angles.

3

Shape into a pack

She writes it to social/queue/ or the video schedule. You reply approve.

4

Produce the media

Hand-write it, render it, or shoot it — per the tier you landed on.

5

Post

Media to phone, paste the caption, publish, stay for the first hour.
